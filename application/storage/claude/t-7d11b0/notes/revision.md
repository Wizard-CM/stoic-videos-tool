# Revision: Max gets a small blue backpack over one shoulder

## Feedback
"Give him a small blue backpack over one shoulder."

## Decision
- One-shoulder sling over the screen-right shoulder (his left; the lit side, so the blue reads brightest).
- The pack hangs behind him, tilted ~10 deg with its top leaning toward the neck. From the front we see:
  - its rounded dome top, grab-loop handle, zipper seam and yellow-grey zipper pull rising above the shoulder line
    between the neck and the arm;
  - a sliver of its outer side beside the upper arm (more of it whenever the right arm lifts).
- One padded strap comes over the top of that shoulder, in front of the hoodie, with a grey buckle, and runs down
  under the upper arm. It stays clear of the purple-hex and blue-heart stickers.
- Size: about 150 x 235 canvas units (half the head width): clearly "small".
- Colour: a true cobalt blue, brighter and bluer than the teal hoodie so it never merges with it.

## Palette (kit/style.json, added only)
- pack `#3D7FE6`, pack_shade `#2C5FBF` (flat shadow on the side away from the upper-right light),
  pack_dark `#1F4590` (zipper seam, handle), pack_hi `#8AB8FF` (one highlight stroke on the dome).
- Buckle and zipper pull reuse `grommet`.

## Files / parts touched
- kit/style.json: palette entries above.
- kit/parts.json `body`: the pack group inserted FIRST (so the torso covers it), the strap + buckle appended LAST
  (over the shoulder outline).
- Nothing else changes: same head, face, hair, arms, hands, gestures, expressions, mouths, eyes, names.
